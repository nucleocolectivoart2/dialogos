# **App Name**: Núcleo Vivo

## Core Features:

- Podcast Playback Interface: Provide a user-friendly interface to browse and play podcast episodes directly on the platform, leveraging Spotify embeds or a custom audio player for RSS-derived content.
- Dynamic Episode Feed Integration: Automatically fetch and display the latest podcast episodes from the RSS feed, showing titles, snippets, and links to listen, creating a 'living ecosystem of sound'.
- Interactive Conversation Map: A dynamic, non-linear visualization that connects episodes, key themes, invited guests, and territories, allowing users to explore relationships within the sustainability ecosystem.
- Episode Detail & Archive Pages: Each podcast episode will have a dedicated page, serving as a 'living archive' that includes full descriptions, relevant metadata, and potential connections to other content within the ecosystem.
- AI-Powered Episode Insights Tool: Utilize generative AI as a tool to process podcast episode content (from RSS data), offering features like summaries, extraction of key tensions, identification of sustainable ideas, and mapping relationships between different actors or themes mentioned.
- Content Storage and Indexing System: A backend system to store and index parsed data from podcast RSS feeds, including episode titles, descriptions, audio links, and metadata, to facilitate the interactive map and AI features.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary background color: A soft, creamy yellow (#EEE8B2) to provide a warm and inviting base.
- Accent and highlight color: A rich bronze/tan (#C18D52) for interactive elements and sophisticated touches, inspired by the gold veins in the reference image.
- Main text and very dark elements: A deep, almost black, dark green (#081B1B) for strong contrast and readability.
- Primary green color: A refined, deep forest green (#203B37) for main components, headers, and thematic sections, symbolizing nature and depth.
- Secondary green shade: A medium sage green (#5A8F76) for secondary elements, cards, or subtle differentiations.
- Lightest green shade: A gentle, light mint green (#96CDB0) for backgrounds of specific sections, hover states, or subtle accents.
- Headline font: 'Alegreya' (humanist serif) to convey an elegant, intellectual, and narrative-driven tone, suitable for reflecting deep conversations and conceptual thought.
- Body text font: 'Inter' (grotesque-style sans-serif) for its modern, clean, and highly readable qualities, ensuring clarity across all content.
- Icons should be modern, clean, and reflect themes of interconnectedness, growth, collaboration, and dialogue. Prioritize circular or organic shapes where appropriate to emphasize the 'living ecosystem' concept.
- The layout will emphasize connectivity and exploration, with elements designed to facilitate navigation through complex, non-linear 'conversation maps' and related content. Prioritize clear hierarchies and digestible information chunks.
- Incorporate subtle and smooth transition animations for elements such as navigation, loading states, and when interacting with the conversation map, to enhance the 'living system' feel and user engagement.
